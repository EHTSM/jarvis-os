## Why no dialog opened

Two independent reasons, both measured:

1. **The dialog triggers are not rendered at mobile widths.** `.cmd-pulse-stop`
   lives in the Command Center pulse bar; at 390/430 the pulse row collapses and
   the emergency-stop control is not present in the DOM.
2. **The data-driven dialogs need data the audit credential cannot reach.**
   14 of 17 failed responses across the walk were
   `403 {"error":"Not a member of this workspace"}`, so contact, plan and agent
   detail dialogs have nothing to open onto.
